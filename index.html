Who am I

A self-taught marketer, designer and builder from Gujarat. I lead marketing at an export manufacturer, run a brand and growth company, and consult founders on how to grow.

“26 saal ka hoon, lekin meri skill 10+ saal purani hai.”

I grew up in a small village with no computers and no roadmap, only curiosity. I taught myself photo editing, design, video, blogging and YouTube years before any of it was a career in India.

In 2022 I joined Micson Corporation as a Social Media Manager. Today I'm its Chief Marketing Officer, and I also run iboxverse, a brand and growth company that brings strategy, design, marketing and technology together for 50+ brands.

I didn't start because it was trending. I started when nobody knew this could pay.

The journey

Skills compound. Here's the proof.

What I learned at 15 pays me at 25. Every step below was self-taught.

2015

One PC, one Android phone, endless curiosity

Learned Photoshop and PicsArt, editing photos, designing logos and making posters for friends and Facebook pages.

2016

Started a YouTube channel

Editing tutorials recorded on my own screen, back when Jio had just made data free and YouTube wasn't a career yet in India.

College

Blogs, two channels and my first team

Built Blogger websites and co-founded two educational YouTube channels with friends. Both crossed 1,000 subscribers.

2022

Marketing Head, then my own work

Joined Micson Corporation as Social Media Manager and made my mark in 90 days. Then started managing social media for companies and hospitals from Jamnagar.

2023

AI from day one

Adopted ChatGPT on launch day and built ERP, CRM and billing systems on Google Sheets and Apps Script.

2026

CMO, founder and MBA student

Chief Marketing Officer at Micson Corporation, co-founder of iboxverse, founder of RaviTech Ventures, partner to 50+ brands, and pursuing an MBA in Digital Marketing Management.

What I believe in

Six rules I work by.

Skills compound

Small skills learned early become big advantages later. Start before you feel ready.

Hunger beats location

You don't need a big city. You need a big hunger and an internet connection.

Business before likes

Marketing is measured in qualified enquiries and revenue, not vanity numbers.

AI is a multiplier

AI won't replace marketers. Marketers who use AI will replace those who don't.

Systems over hustle

Good dashboards, CRMs and processes let a small team do the work of a big one.

Grow people with you

The real goal is connecting more people to meaningful work, from my village to the world.
